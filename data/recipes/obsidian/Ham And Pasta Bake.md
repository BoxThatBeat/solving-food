## Ingredients
- 2 cups (500 mL) elbow macaroni
- 3 qts. (3 L) boiling water
- 1 tbsp. (15 mL) cooking oil (optional)
- 2 tsp. (10 mL) salt
- ¼ cup (60 mL) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp. (5 mL) salt
- ⅛ tsp. (0.5 mL) pepper
- ½ tsp. (2 mL) dry mustard
- 1 tbsp. (15 mL) onion flakes
- 2½ cups (625 mL) milk
- 10 oz. (300 g) frozen chopped broccoli
- Boiling water, to cover
- 2 cups (500 mL) diced or cubed cooked ham (about ¾ lb., 340 g)
- ¾ cup (175 mL) grated medium or sharp Cheddar cheese

Topping:
- 2 tbsp. (30 mL) hard margarine (or butter)
- ½ cup (125 mL) fine dry bread crumbs

## Instructions
1. Cook macaroni in first amount of boiling water, cooking oil and salt in large, uncovered, saucepan for 5 to 7 minutes until tender but firm. Drain.
2. Combine flour, salt, pepper, dry mustard and onion flakes in medium saucepan. Gradually mix in milk so no lumps remain. Heat and stir until boiling and thickened.
3. Cook broccoli in second amount of boiling water until tender-crisp. Drain. Add to sauce. Stir in macaroni.
4. Add ham and cheese. Stir. Turn into ungreased 2 quart (2 L) casserole dish.
5. Topping: Melt margarine in saucepan. Stir in bread crumbs. Sprinkle over all. Bake in 350°F (175°C) oven for about 30 minutes until browned. Serves 6.
