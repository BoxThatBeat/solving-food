## Ingredients
- 8 ounces cavatappi pasta or pasta of choice
- 2 pints cherry tomatoes
- 8 ounce block feta cheese
- ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- 2 garlic cloves minced
- ¼ cup fresh basil packed and chopped, plus more for serving

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Place cherry tomatoes in an oven-safe baking dish. Pour olive oil on top and season with salt and pepper. Toss until combined.
2. Place the feta block in the middle of the baking dish surrounded by tomatoes, flipping a couple times to coat with oil and seasoning.
3. Bake for 35 minutes until tomatoes burst and feta melts.
4. Cook pasta in salted water according to package directions until al dente. Drain, reserving ½ cup cooking liquid.
5. Immediately add minced garlic and basil to the tomato and feta mixture and toss to combine.
6. Transfer cooked pasta to the baking dish and toss together. Garnish with fresh basil and serve warm.
