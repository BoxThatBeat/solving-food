## Ingredients
- 1lb ground turkey (I prefer 93/7 lean)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 large red onion
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs or [almond flour](https://amzn.to/2R73fbL)
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh dill
- 1/2 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 2 tbsp [olive oil](https://amzn.to/2URYp0T)

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, add ground turkey and minced garlic.
2. Grate 1/4 red onion into turkey, alternatively chop red onion very small.
3. Add egg, breadcrumbs, dill, oregano, salt, and pepper. Mix until just combined, I use my hands.
4. Form into 14 meatballs.
5. Heat sauce pan with [olive oil](https://amzn.to/2URYp0T) over medium heat. Once hot, add meatballs and cook for 7-8 minutes, turning every constantly to ensure even cooking on all sides. Once meatballs reach 165F they are done.
6. While meatballs are cooking, make [[Tzatziki Sauce]]
7. Serve over rice!
