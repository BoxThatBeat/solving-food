## Ingredients
**Muffins:**
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 1 1/2 cups old-fashioned oats
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 cup canola oil
- 1/2 cup brown sugar (light or dark)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup vanilla-flavored Greek yogurt
- 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

**Streusel:**
- 2 tablespoons canola oil
- 1/3 cup brown sugar (light or dark)
- 1/2 cup old-fashioned oats
- 1/3 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 12-cup muffin pan with cooking spray.
2. Whisk together flour, oats, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon in a large bowl.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together oil, brown sugar, eggs, vanilla, and Greek yogurt. Combine wet and dry ingredients until incorporated. Fold in 1 cup chocolate chips.
4. Divide batter evenly among muffin cups, filling almost full.
5. Mix streusel ingredients with a fork until coated. Place a heaping tablespoon over each muffin.
6. Bake 15-18 minutes until golden brown. Cool in pan 5-10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
