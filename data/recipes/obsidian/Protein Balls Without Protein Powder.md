## Ingredients
- 2 cups (200 g) old fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup (80 g) unsweetened coconut flakes
- 1 cup (160 g) hulled raw hemp seeds
- ½ cup (60 g) oat flour
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 1½ cups (384 g) smooth peanut butter
- ½ cup (168 g) honey
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 4 ounces miniature chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Optional: Toast oats, coconut, and seeds on a baking sheet at 300°F for approximately 15 minutes, stirring once, to intensify flavors.
2. In a large bowl, combine oats, coconut flakes, hemp seeds, oat flour, and salt.
3. Create a well in the dry ingredients and add peanut butter, honey, and vanilla extract. Mix thoroughly and let cool for at least 10 minutes.
4. Once cooled, fold in chocolate chips to distribute evenly throughout the mixture.
5. Using an ice cream scoop, portion the mixture into heaping tablespoons onto a parchment-lined baking sheet (approximately 36 portions).
6. Roll each portion tightly into a ball between your palms.
7. Refrigerate until firm and serve chilled.
