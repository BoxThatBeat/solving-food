## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (125 mL) butter or margarine, softened
- 1 cup (250 mL) brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup (250 mL) canned pumpkin (without spice)
- 2 cups (500 mL) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp. (7 mL) baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. (2 mL) baking soda
- 1/2 tsp. (2 mL) salt
- 1/2 tsp. (2 mL) cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp. (2 mL) nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp. (2 mL) ginger
- 1 cup (250 mL) semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Cream butter and sugar in mixing bowl. Beat in eggs one at a time until smooth. Stir in pumpkin.
2. In another bowl combine remaining 9 ingredients. Stir to mix then pour into mixing bowl. Stir all together just until moistened.
3. Spoon into muffin tin
4. Bake at 425°F for 5 minutes and then 350°F 14 minutes
5. 10 minutes cooling time

## Notes
- Yield: 1 loaf
