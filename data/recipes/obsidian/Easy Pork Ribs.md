## Ingredients
- 3 lb (1.5 kg) Ontario side or back pork ribs
- 1 Tbsp (15 mL) freshly ground pepper
- 1 tsp (5 mL) red pepper flakes
- 2 tsp (10 mL) dried thyme leaves
- 1 to 2 cups (250 to 500 mL) your favourite BBQ sauce

## Instructions
1. For back ribs, preheat to 325°F (160°C) and remove tough membrane on non-meaty side of ribs. For side ribs, trim visible fat and preheat to 325°F (160°C).
2. Combine black pepper, red pepper flakes and thyme in a bowl. Season ribs, place meaty-side up on baking sheets.
3. Add hot water to create approximately 1/4-inch depth. Cover with foil and bake at 325°F until meat recedes from rib tips (about 1 hour for back ribs, 2 hours for side ribs).
4. Remove ribs and discard liquid. Proceed to browning or refrigerate up to 3 days.
5. **Oven method:** Increase heat to 450°F (230°C). Brush sauce on one side, cook 5 minutes until bubbly and golden. Flip, apply remaining sauce, cook 5 more minutes.
6. **Grill method:** Preheat to high for 10 minutes, reduce to medium. Grill on medium-low heat for 15 minutes, brushing with sauce and turning occasionally.
