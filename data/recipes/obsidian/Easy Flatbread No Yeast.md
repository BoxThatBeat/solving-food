## Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (260g)
- 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon pure maple syrup or sugar
- ¾ cup cold water (175ml)
- Avocado oil for cooking

## Instructions
1. Mix dry ingredients: Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Create dough: Make a well in the center, add olive oil, maple syrup, and most of the water. Stir with a rubber spatula until combined, adding remaining water if needed.
3. Knead and rest: Transfer to floured surface and knead 5-10 times until smooth. Cover and rest 10 minutes.
4. Roll out: Divide dough into six pieces. Dust with flour and roll each into a disc ⅛-¼ inch thick.
5. Cook: Heat 1-2 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook flatbreads until golden (1-2 minutes per side).
6. Keep warm: Transfer to a plate, cover with towel to retain warmth.
