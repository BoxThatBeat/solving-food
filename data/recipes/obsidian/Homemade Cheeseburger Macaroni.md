## Ingredients
- 1 pound lean ground beef
- 1/2 cup finely diced onion (or 1 teaspoon onion powder)
- 2 cups milk
- 1 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 1 1/2 cups elbow macaroni
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 3/4 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. Combine cornstarch, garlic powder, sugar, salt, paprika, and pepper in a small bowl; set aside.
2. Brown ground beef and onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon until fully cooked. Drain excess fat.
3. Add milk, broth, and uncooked macaroni to the beef. Stir in the spice mixture. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
4. Once boiling, reduce heat to a gentle boil, cover, and simmer 9-12 minutes until pasta is tender, stirring occasionally. Add more broth or water if needed.
5. Remove from heat and stir in cheese until completely melted. Serve immediately.
