## Ingredients
- 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 cup 100% pumpkin, canned
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil or melted coconut oil
- 2 Tbsp. honey
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp. nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp. pumpkin spice
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1/8 tsp. turmeric
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 1 cup dark chocolate chips (3/4 cup to mix in batter, remainder for topping)
- Optional topping: pepitas (pumpkin seeds)

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 425°F and coat muffin tin with non-stick spray.
2. Using a food processor or blender, pulse oats for approximately 15 seconds until partially ground.
3. Add remaining ingredients except pepitas and chocolate chips. Blend until smooth. Fold in 3/4 cup chocolate chips, reserving remainder for topping.
4. Distribute batter into prepared muffin cups, filling three-quarters full. Top with pepitas and remaining chocolate chips.
5. Bake 15-18 minutes until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool briefly before serving.
