## Ingredients
**Wet Ingredients**
- 3 bananas, mashed (about 1 cup mashed)
- 2 eggs, at room temperature
- 1/3 cup avocado oil (can substitute vegetable or canola oil)
- 1/3 cup maple syrup or honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Dry Ingredients**
- 1 3/4 cups oat flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line a muffin pan with muffin liners.
2. Combine mashed bananas, eggs, avocado oil, maple syrup, and vanilla extract in a large mixing bowl and mix until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, mix together oat flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
4. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and gently fold together until combined.
5. Add chocolate chips and mix together.
6. Spoon batter into muffin pan, filling each liner 3/4 of the way full.
7. Bake for 18-22 minutes.
